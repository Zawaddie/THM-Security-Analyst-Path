## My TakeHomes in the Room:

**Security Hierarchy**

Cyber security priorities are different for every company. 

<img width="841" height="313" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7d6fff73-7b5a-4146-9cb6-d36d81dc45eb" />

There are different teams in a Company's security team:

- **Red Team:** Offensive security experts, pentesters, or ethical hackers who look for security issues

- **GRC Team:** Specialists managing policies and ensuring compliance with regulations like PCI DSS

- **Blue Team:** Defensive security experts like SOC analysts, engineers, or incident responders

2. **Cyber Incident Response Team (CIRT)**

If SOC expertise is not enough or the incident goes out of control, you urgently call the "firefighters" - CIRT, also called CSIRT or CERT. 
The members should have a broad knowledge of cyber threats and handle breaches without depending on tools like EDR or SIEM.
