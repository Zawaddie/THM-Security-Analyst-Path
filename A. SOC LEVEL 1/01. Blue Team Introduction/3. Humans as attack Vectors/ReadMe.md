# Humans as attack Vectors

# My TakeHomes from the Room:

- Humans labeled the weakest cyber security element.
- Humans are targeted because of the access they can provide - to websites, mailboxes, or databases.

Attacks targeting humans share a common trait: they rely on manipulating the victim into helping the attacker, whether knowingly or not.

This tactic is known as **social engineering,** and it works by exploiting human psychology rather than technical flaw.

**human attacks include:**

- Phishing Attacks
- Malware Downloads using fake CAPTCHAs, malicious QR codes, and SEO poisoning.
- Deepfakes using AI generated videos and audios.
- Impersonation
- USB drop campaigns, physical attacks, insider threats,and even fake job offers

Defending against threats involves two key tasks: 
- **Mitigation**: aims to prevent or reduce the chance and impact of attack
- **Detection.**:  SOC skills are vital to detect and investigate advanced attacks that slip through
